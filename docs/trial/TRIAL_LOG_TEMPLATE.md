Scales:
- **Starting ease:** "How easy was it to begin the task you chose?" 1 very difficult · 5 very easy · N/A no attempt.
- **App pressure:** "How much pressure or guilt did the app itself add?" 0 none · 4 a lot · N/A app not used.

**Pause signal:** two used days with app pressure 3–4 → consider pausing. You can stop any time for any reason.

## Weekly review — 10 minutes max, once

1. Median starting ease: baseline ___ · trial ___
2. Median app pressure: baseline ___ · trial ___
3. Did it help me begin? ___
4. Did it add pressure? ___
5. Did it take time away from the thing I wanted to do (Flutter, work, rest)? ___
6. Anything different this week (exams, workload, new project)? ___

Decision (circle one): **Keep** · **Simplify** · **Remove** · **Inconclusive**

Guide: keep if ease went up by about 1 point, pressure did not go up, and you want to continue. This is a rough
decision aid, not a scientific test.
